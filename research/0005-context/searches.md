## Why this is a limitation and not merely a note

Two consequences a reviewer should weigh:

1. **This report cannot have found anything R-001 and R-0004 missed.** Its
   contribution is arrangement and argument — the composition claim in §5, the
   four-forcing-function frame in §3, and the conjunction table in §7 — not new
   evidence. Where it appears to add a fact, it is quoting one of them.
2. **Anything that changed in the world after R-001's retrieval pass
   (2026-09-26) and R-0004's (2026-10-10) is invisible here.** The statutory
   material in §3.2 is the most time-sensitive and the most likely to have
   moved.

If D3 context is later taken past draft, **a retrieval pass is the thing to
add**, and §3.2 is where it would pay.
